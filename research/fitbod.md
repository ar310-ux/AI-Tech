# Fitbod

**Category:** Adaptive strength workout planner  
**Research date:** 2026-09-22  
**Website:** https://fitbod.me/

## What it offers
Fitbod positions itself as a personalized workout planner that creates strength plans around a user's goals, fitness level, and available equipment. Its central promise is adaptation rather than a fixed program.

## Competitive strengths
- Strong overlap with Form / Forge's goal, experience, and equipment inputs.
- Personalization is the headline value proposition.
- Established fitness-app positioning and a dedicated strength focus.

## Gaps and opportunity for Form / Forge
Form / Forge can be more approachable by producing a clear weekly guide immediately, with less setup and less emphasis on exhaustive exercise tracking. A calm, editorial plan format is a potential differentiator from an algorithm-first experience.

## Watch
- How much of the experience requires an account or paid subscription.
- Whether plan changes are understandable to beginners.
- The level of exercise detail and progression guidance.
