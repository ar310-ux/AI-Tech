# Boostcamp

**Category:** Workout tracker and coach-designed program marketplace  
**Research date:** 2026-09-22  
**Website:** https://www.boostcamp.app/

## What it offers
Boostcamp presents itself as a free workout tracker and program app with set, rep, and PR logging, built-in auto-progression, and more than 11,000 programs for beginner through advanced lifters. The site highlights coach-designed programs and says programs have no paywall.

## Competitive strengths
- Very large program supply and broad level coverage.
- Combines discovery, tracking, and progression in one product.
- A free-access message lowers the barrier to trying programs.

## Gaps and opportunity for Form / Forge
Form / Forge can be intentionally small and opinionated: one useful week based on the user's actual equipment and availability. The opportunity is reducing choice overload for users who want a starting point rather than a marketplace.

## Watch
- How program quality and relevance are surfaced among thousands of options.
- Whether free programs include enough beginner guidance.
- How much auto-progression depends on detailed logging.
