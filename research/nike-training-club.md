# Nike Training Club

**Category:** Guided workout and training-content library  
**Research date:** 2026-09-22  
**Website:** https://www.nike.com/ntc-app

## What it offers
Nike Training Club promotes more than 185 workouts, including bodyweight sessions, yoga, targeted training programs, and full-equipment home workouts for different fitness levels.

## Competitive strengths
- Broad content range across formats, goals, and experience levels.
- Strong brand trust and polished guided-session experience.
- Bodyweight and home-equipment coverage overlaps with Form / Forge's intended audience.

## Gaps and opportunity for Form / Forge
Form / Forge can be more decisive: instead of asking users to browse a large catalog, it can convert a few constraints into a short weekly route. A generated schedule can complement, rather than compete with, a content-heavy library.

## Watch
- How users choose between a library and a personalized plan.
- Whether the app handles weekly scheduling or primarily individual sessions.
- Which content is current and how often programs are refreshed.
