# Strong

**Category:** Workout tracker and strength-training planner  
**Research date:** 2026-09-22  
**Website:** https://www.strong.app/

## What it offers
Strong describes itself as an intuitive workout tracker and strength-training planner. The product is centered on recording gym sessions and helping users stay on track over time.

## Competitive strengths
- Clear habit and progress-tracking use case.
- Strong credibility signal: the official site says it is trusted by more than 3 million people.
- Likely compelling for users who already know what they want to lift.

## Gaps and opportunity for Form / Forge
Form / Forge can own the earlier moment in the journey: turning an outcome, schedule, and equipment list into a usable first week. The contrast is plan creation before detailed logging, with a lower cognitive load for people who do not yet have a routine.

## Watch
- How much of the product is tracker-first versus plan-first.
- Whether novice users receive enough exercise coaching.
- Which progression and history features are gated.
